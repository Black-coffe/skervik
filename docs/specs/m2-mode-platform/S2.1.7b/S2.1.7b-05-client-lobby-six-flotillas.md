# Client lobby: preset-aware bot cap and two new flotilla identities

## Goal

The lobby stops assuming a 4-seat room. `MAX_LOBBY_BOTS` becomes preset-derived
(`maxSeats − 1`, so 3 for Classic and 5 for Grand Chart), the `expanded` preset that story 01 shipped
renders and behaves correctly in the radiogroup, and seats 5–6 get real identities instead of
silently reusing seat 0/1's colors (`flotillaColors.ts:19-24`, `seatIndex % 4`).

## Non-goals
- Do NOT invent flotilla names, colors, or emblems. DESIGN.md §2.2 identities are **owner-locked**;
  the two below are the planner's proposal, pre-authored so no worker improvises. If they feel
  wrong, say so in `## Findings` — do not substitute your own.
- Do NOT add a seat-count control to the lobby. The preset IS the seat choice (plan D3); there is no
  seat field on the wire and there must not be one.
- Do NOT touch `matchTopology.ts`, `GameTable.tsx`, the placement hooks or `BoardScene.ts` —
  stories 03 and 06 own those.
- Do NOT restyle the lobby or the players rail. `PlayersRail.tsx` already renders N seats; it needs
  no change and should get none.
- Do NOT hardcode 6 anywhere. Every cap comes from the selected profile's `maxSeats`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `MAX_LOBBY_BOTS` (`lobbyStore.ts:19`) is replaced by a preset-derived cap
      `loadRuleProfile(profileId).maxSeats − 1`; the stale "mirrors server's wire cap… 4-seat
      Classic room" comment goes with it. Selecting Grand Chart allows up to 5 bots; switching back
      to Classic **clamps** an already-chosen 4–5 bots down to 3 rather than sending an invalid
      selection (a test covers the switch-down clamp specifically).
- [ ] `selectLobbySelection` still sends only `{profileId, bots}` — no new wire field.
- [ ] `expanded` appears in the preset radiogroup via `SHIPPING_PROFILE_IDS` with its existing
      trilingual copy (`lobby.preset.expanded.*`, already in all 3 locales). Any lobby test that
      asserted exactly 4 options is updated to 5 — this is the transient story 01 knowingly left red.
- [ ] `FLOTILLAS` has 6 entries and `flotillaForSeat` cycles on `FLOTILLAS.length`, not `4`. Seats 4
      and 5 resolve to the two NEW identities; seats 0–3 are byte-unchanged, so every existing
      Classic color/emblem expectation still passes.
- [ ] The two new identities are added exactly as specified (planner proposal, owner may override):

      | Flotilla | Token | OKLCH | sRGB | Emblem |
      |---|---|---|---|---|
      | Мурена / Moray / Мурена | `--fl-moray` | `oklch(0.62 0.13 145)` | `#4f9257` | moray silhouette |
      | Манта / Manta / Манта | `--fl-manta` | `oklch(0.55 0.14 330)` | `#a1548f` | manta ray silhouette |

- [ ] Trilingual per ADR-0008: flotilla labels exist in en/ru/uk with the names above, and the two
      identities are appended to DESIGN.md §2.2's table and the lore-primer glossary (CLAUDE.md:
      every new user-facing term is authored in 3 languages at creation).
- [ ] A11y invariant holds: each new flotilla has a distinct emblem glyph readable at 14px, and no
      code path shows color without emblem. Note the deutan/protan reasoning in
      `## Implementation notes` (moray 145 @ L0.62 vs narwhal 190 @ L0.78 separate by lightness).
- [ ] i18n coverage test (if one enumerates keys across locales) stays green — no locale missing a key.
