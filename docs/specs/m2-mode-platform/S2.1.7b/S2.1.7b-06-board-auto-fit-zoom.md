# S2.7.2: board auto-fit zoom (makes the 37-tile board fit at all)

## Goal

The board's default (un-panned) view scales its actual field extent to the available chart box
instead of rendering at a fixed `scale = 1`. This closes S2.7.1 finding F1 (at 1280px the board
clips the trade dock by ~24px, ~55px with port markers — unfixable at scale 1 by any offset) AND is
what lets the radius-3 expanded board fit on screen at all: at 37 tiles the field is far wider than
the radius-2 ~360px the current constants assume. Implements
`docs/specs/m2-mode-platform/S2.7.2-board-auto-fit-zoom.md` — read that spec first; it is the
authority, this story is its dispatch.

## Non-goals
- Do NOT re-derive the topology. Story 03 landed `matchTopology.ts` / `useMatchTopology()`; consume
  it. Fit math reads the field extent from the descriptors, never from a radius constant and never
  from a hardcoded 19-tile size (S2.7.2 "Notes", forward-looking bullet).
- Do NOT remount the Pixi scene on resize or dock toggle — same constraint as S2.7.1. Reuse the
  existing `resizeTo: mountEl` and the `setDockVisible` seam.
- Do NOT rebuild pan/zoom, add gestures, or touch the mobile/≤1024 bottom-sheet reflow (its own
  story). Desktop ≥1280px only.
- Do NOT touch the lobby, flotilla colors, hit-testing, or `boardModel.ts`.
- Do NOT leave `TRADE_DOCK_OFFSET_PX = 204` alive alongside the new math — S2.7.2 says the fit
  SUPERSEDES it. Two competing offset systems is the failure mode here.

- [ ] **S2.7.2 AC1:** a computed-extent unit test proves that at 1280px chart width with the dock

- [ ] **S2.7.2 AC2:** fit recomputes on window resize AND on dock toggle, with no scene remount and
      without clobbering an in-progress user pan/zoom. Pick the simpler of the two documented

      than left as a radius-2 constant, so the sea ring surrounds the expanded board too.

## Implementation notes

- New pure module `board/boardFit.ts`: `computeFieldExtent(topology)` (tile corners at true
  `HEX_SIZE`, not the gap-shrunk render size, plus port-marker footprints — mirrors
  `pieceModel.ts`'s port-offset math, duplicated rather than imported so this stays Pixi-free),
  `fitFieldToBox(extent, box, options?)` (returns `{scale, x, y}`, capped at `MAX_FIT_SCALE = 1.5`),
  and `seaRadiusForExtent(extent)` (half bbox diagonal + margin — grows for the 37-tile board).
  `BoardScene.ts` computes `fieldExtent`/`seaRadius` once per scene (topology is stable for the
  scene's lifetime) and feeds them into `buildSea`, the mist circle, and a `recomputeFit()` closure
  that sets `world.scale`/`world.position`.
- AC2 policy chosen: **reset-to-fit** on both window resize and dock toggle (not "fit once on mount,
  then leave pan/zoom alone"). Reused the existing `resizeTo: mountEl` wiring — no new listener;
  `app.renderer.on('resize', recomputeFit)` fires off Pixi's own `ResizePlugin` (`renderer.resize()`
  emits `'resize'`). Reset was picked over preserve-on-resize because the bug this story closes (F1:
  board clips the dock at 1280px) must also stay fixed if a user resizes down to 1280px live, not
  just at mount — a preserve policy would let F1 reappear post-mount. It's also the simpler of the
  two (no "has the user interacted" state to track). Tradeoff: an in-progress manual pan/zoom is
  reset on any resize/dock-toggle, same acceptance the owner already gave the pre-fit 1280px overlap
  — pan/zoom is the recovery mechanism, not the steady state.
- `TRADE_DOCK_OFFSET_PX` removed; `TRADE_DOCK_RESERVE_PX` (still 408px, S2.7.1's dock geometry) now
  only sizes the fit box's reserved left edge, never a raw position shift.
- `GameTable.tsx`/`GameScreen.tsx` needed no changes — the fit lives entirely inside
  `createBoardScene`/`setDockVisible`, both already wired through the existing S2.7.1 seam.
